Printable reports

Download PDF is the browser's print (DS-147). Sheets are US Letter at real size on screen and on paper, built from data with row budgets. Guide: Printable reports.

Print preview

The preview is portalled to <body> as the print root. Its Print to PDF button sets body.saga-printing, names the file through document.title, and prints only the sheets: page 1 portrait, page 2 landscape. In Safari and on iPhone or iPad, the note under the toolbar appears, because WebKit prints every sheet portrait (DS-153).

Sheets

.saga-sheet (portrait) and .saga-sheet--landscape, with a header, a body that clips, and the optional footer. Stats, the report table and the continued line on the first; a --grid --small table on the second.
