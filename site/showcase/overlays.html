Overlays

Modal panels on a navy scrim (DS-012), multi-step headers, toasts, popover menus, tooltips and inline notices. Layering follows the z-index ladder (DS-013).

Use a native <dialog>: showModal() gives the scrim via ::backdrop, focus trapping and Escape for free. The same classes work on a div inside .saga-scrim.

Framed shell with a local scrim and toast stack

A .saga-shell--framed fills its positioned frame instead of the viewport (a decision-board scene, a preview). Inside it, .saga-scrim--local and .saga-toast-stack--local are absolute to the frame, so overlays stay in the scene. A native <dialog> always goes to the top layer, so a framed scene uses the div form (DS-118).

Toasts

Bottom-right stack, navy surface, a 4px colored left edge (DS-102, DS-115). Dwell is 3500ms + 50ms per character, clamped to 4–15s. Enter and exit animate opacity and transform only; under reduced motion the rise collapses and the fade stays.
