Buttons

Flat, 6px radius, semibold (DS-017). Blue is the only action color; teal is never a button fill (DS-003). Disabled is a fill, not opacity.

Variants

<button class="saga-btn">Save</button>
<button class="saga-btn saga-btn--secondary">Cancel</button>
<button class="saga-btn saga-btn--quiet">Quiet</button>
<button class="saga-btn saga-btn--danger">Delete</button>

<button class="saga-btn saga-btn--caution">Yes, move them</button>
<button class="saga-btn saga-btn--primary">Primary (alias)</button>

.saga-btn alone is primary; .saga-btn--primary is an explicit alias for pages that name every intent. --caution is the amber confirm fill on --color-warning-strong (DS-112), used only as the confirming button of a caution dialog.

Pressed and status
